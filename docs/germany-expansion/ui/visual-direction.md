# Germany visual direction — proposal for review

This document specifies direction, not final marketing copy or implementation. Read [evidence limits](UI-WEB-001-reference-audit.md): competitor rendered styling and mobile interaction remain unverified. All numerical design budgets below are proposed acceptance targets, not measurements of existing sites.

## A. Design North Star

A calm, capable tutoring coordinator who helps a learner or family find the right language support. Preserve SPM's professional warmth: premium through clarity, care and evidence, never exclusivity. The three pillars are TRUST + ATTRACTION, USER EXPERIENCE and SEARCHABILITY. Make the offering understandable before asking visitors to commit or create an account.

## B. First-impression strategy

Within the opening content establish: SPM identity, language tutoring in the Germany context, the four launch services, and the next action. Use a concise headline, one short support sentence, Free Trial and WhatsApp actions, and one credible learning image. Do not use headline statistics, guaranteed outcomes, several floating badges or a full multi-step form as the first visual demand. Children and adults must both find relevance; do not make every phrase parent-only.

## C. Trust strategy and evidence model

Show verified operational proof before decorative stars: who coordinates, how a tutor is selected, what the trial does and how a concern is handled. Combine the proposed “Why Families Choose SPM” and teacher-quality blocks; broaden the label to learners and families. Keep no more than three proof-backed quality points, with optional detail.

| Topic | Verifiable proof needed | Future presentation | Until confirmed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Teacher selection | Dated recruitment criteria and evidence of application review | Plain process steps with owner | Do not assert rigorous selection |
| Qualifications | Actual certificates/degrees, subject relevance, holder permission | Specific qualification next to consented tutor identity | No generic certified/elite/native claim |
| Experience | Documented relevant teaching history and age/subject scope | Short factual summary | No invented years or student totals |
| Vetting | Defined checks, completed records, jurisdiction/scope and review date | Accurate description of checks actually performed | Do not equate interview with background clearance |
| Matching | Confirmed intake criteria and coordinator workflow | Three-step explanation with realistic availability limits | No guaranteed perfect match |
| Quality monitoring | Actual feedback/review cadence and escalation process | What learner receives and whom to contact | Do not promise reports or replacement times without operations sign-off |
| Reviews | Original source, date, permission, context and translation approval | Two or three excerpts with provenance | No invented stars, review counts or Germany attribution |

A source-code string is a MARKETING CLAIM, not VERIFIABLE PROOF. Current NA statistics and reviews cannot automatically be labeled German outcomes. Competitor statistics and badges are not transferable. Business owner must approve each claim and its evidence before publication.

## D. Conversion strategy

One canonical trial-request journey. Header, hero and later primary CTAs lead to the same future form; choosing a service may preselect it without hiding alternatives. A coordinator follows up to understand fit and arrange a trial; this is a proposed operational contract requiring confirmation, not an instant booking guarantee.

Candidate form: contact name; preferred contact channel plus its value; tutoring language; learner type (self/child or student); optional short goal. Keep 4–5 visible essential controls depending on channel selection. Collect detailed academic background later. UI language and tutoring language are independent. No password, account, payment, calendar, school documents or precise birth date in the landing journey.

Specify honest states for the later implementation: submitting, confirmed receipt, retryable error and WhatsApp fallback. Show success only after an actual accepted request. Do not reuse the current simulated EnrollmentCard success. Include a concise privacy explanation and reviewed policy link; do not preselect marketing consent. Any promised response time, free-trial duration, eligibility or capacity must be business-approved. No workflow is implemented here.

## E. Proposed section order and full content model

This reduces the original fourteen blocks to twelve purposeful units. If review evidence is unavailable, omit that proof block rather than create placeholders.

### Use-case evaluation — concepts, not final copy

| Scenario | What to clarify | Visual role | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arabic-speaking family in Germany; child needs German/school-language support | Preferred communication language, learner stage and actual tutoring need | Everyday parent/child learning scene; 2–3 short lines | No deficit stereotypes or guarantee of curriculum/accreditation coverage |
| Student seeking English or French support | Equal language availability and learning objective | Student working with tutor/materials | French is core launch scope, not secondary or coming soon |
| Adult learning German, English, Arabic or French | Self-directed inquiry and suitability assessment | Adult learner in ordinary professional/home setting | No child-only form labels, guaranteed exam outcomes or assumed professional certification |

Use cases should help self-recognition, not pretend to be real customer stories. No invented names, outcomes or testimonial styling.

## F. Content-density rules

One purpose per section. One H1; short descriptive H2s. Prefer 1–2 sentences per paragraph and no more than three bullets per benefit block. Aim for 45–65 characters per line on desktop; do not force equal line counts across languages. Keep long explanations out of service cards. Avoid truncating essential service names, form errors or legal choices. Progressive disclosure may reveal detail, but core offer, terms and next action must be visible without interaction. The budgets above are maximum drafting guides; review clarity rather than padding copy to fit them.

## G. CTA hierarchy

Primary: **Free Trial**. Use one strong SPM treatment consistently; primary styling must not imply instant confirmed booking. Secondary: **WhatsApp**, visually distinct and recognizable without competing size/glow. Text links: process, evidence and FAQ. Repeat primary access in header, hero and form context; do not add a different competing action to every section. No visible Germany contact button is created in this unit. The approved contact values are recorded in [scope freeze](v1-scope-freeze.md).

## H. Imagery direction

Use licensed or consented everyday learning photography: child with guardian where relevant, student, adult learner and real tutor/coordinator if available. Warm eye-level framing, uncluttered backgrounds, room for translated copy. Do not use appearance to imply nationality or qualifications. Avoid staged achievement certificates, stock people presented as staff, overly childish illustrations or embedded UI/text. No competitor images, illustrations or logos.

## I. Card direction

Four equal service cards, not tutor-marketplace listings. Use restrained borders and one consistent radius/shadow role. Subject title first, concise learner need second, clear selection affordance only when interactive. Avoid full-card hover motion on static evidence. No public prices, ratings or availability badges unless separately approved. Mobile is one column; desktop may use a four-card row or 2×2 depending on tested translations.

## J. Typography direction

Preserve SPM's DIN identity where licensed and legible. Verify de/en/ar glyphs, diacritics, ß, punctuation and numeric alignment. Use regular/bold as the functional pair; avoid light body text. Candidate body 16–18px; headings fluid and tested at 200% zoom. Use native language names rather than flags alone. Do not introduce a new font family without an explicit design decision and licensing evidence.

## K. Spacing / whitespace direction

Use an 8px-based rhythm with 4px refinements; candidate section spacing 48–64px mobile and 72–96px desktop, adjusted for content. Limit major background changes; white/light surfaces with one navy support/footer area. Keep 16–24px mobile gutters. Avoid a full viewport-height hero that pushes the only usable action below the fold. Resolve existing token mappings before implementation.

## L. Mobile direction

Test 320/390/768 widths, short landscape screens and virtual keyboards. Order content before imagery; service cards stack. Targets at least 44px, inputs approximately 52px, visible focus and appropriate input modes. If a bottom action area is approved later, reserve its space and safe-area inset, hide or reposition it when the form keyboard is open and avoid stacking chat, WhatsApp and back-to-top controls. Menu must support Escape, focus return, scroll lock and clear close control. No auto-rotating proof carousel or hover-dependent information.

## M. Arabic RTL

Set language/direction at the future market-language boundary; this audit changes neither routing nor translations. Use logical start/end properties and stable reading order. Mirror directional navigation arrows, not logos, photos, numerals or all icons. Isolate email, URLs and international phone numbers as LTR; test punctuation and mixed-language service names. Preserve field labels/error association, screen-reader reading order and natural Arabic line height. Do not reuse global /ar links when a future Germany-language switch should stay in its market.

## N. German long words

Allow multi-line headings/buttons/cards; no fixed text heights. Test terms such as Unterrichtskoordination and Sprachunterricht alongside real reviewed copy. Prefer natural editorial shortening; use language-aware hyphenation when appropriate and verify browser behavior. Never use break-all across normal prose or shrink text until unreadable. Test navigation at intermediate widths and 200% zoom. Contact strings may wrap independently.

## O. Patterns to borrow

From [Superprof](https://www.superprof.de/): clear learning-need access and identity-backed evidence. From [GoStudent's process](https://www.gostudent.org/de-de/wie-es-funktioniert/): a short explained trial sequence. From [Populo's tutor explanation](https://www.skolapopulo.cz/blog/lektori-ve-skole-populo-specialiste-ve-svem-oboru): concrete selection/coordination explanation. These adaptations are recommendations; no conversion uplift is claimed.

## P. What not to copy

Superprof: marketplace search, ranking, exact profile cards, imagery, wording or commercial model. GoStudent: classroom product stack, AI/grade claims, page compositions, branded visuals or pricing. Populo: branch/exam architecture, scarcity prompts, certifications or layout. All three: logos, palettes, illustrations, testimonials and protected text. SPM's own brand and the approved service scope remain authoritative.

## Searchability without activation

Future content should answer distinct tutoring-language and audience intents with clear headings, descriptive links and accessible text. No automatic Germany city pages or NA location-schema expansion. Germany stays noindex/disabled under the approved route foundation; language content, hreflang, sitemap and public SEO activation require later authorization. The UI language trio does not include French; French remains a core tutoring service.
