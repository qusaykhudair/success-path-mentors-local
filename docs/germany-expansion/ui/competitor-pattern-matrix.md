## Adopt / adapt / avoid

| Reference | Adopt as a principle | Adapt to SPM | Avoid copying |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Superprof](https://www.superprof.de/) | Make learner need and teacher evidence easy to find | Four-service choice with coordinator matching | Search marketplace, exact card arrangement, provider ranking, prices, portraits and brand |
| [GoStudent process](https://www.gostudent.org/de-de/wie-es-funktioniert/) | Explain the next three actions | A public trial request, not a portal onboarding journey | Classroom/AI ecosystem, promises, numerical claims, layouts or assets |
| [Populo tutor model](https://www.skolapopulo.cz/blog/lektori-ve-skole-populo-specialiste-ve-svem-oboru) | Explain the work behind teaching quality | Verified SPM selection and follow-up process | Certification/employment guarantees, branch structure and exam-specific offer |

## Evidence needed to close U entries

For each site record actual header-link count at both widths, primary CTA location, menu open/closed state, first-step fields and total visible steps without submission, button dimensions, sticky behavior, two consecutive section screenshots, sample cards, FAQ expanded state and footer. Count only rendered elements; do not count duplicate hidden markup as visible UI. Until then, all mobile and competitor visual-system conclusions remain recommendations, not measured findings.
