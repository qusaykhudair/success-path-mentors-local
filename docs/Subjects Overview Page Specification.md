# 12 - Subjects Overview Page Specification

> Project: Success Path Mentors

> Framework: Next.js 15

> Page Type: SEO Landing Page

> Priority: Critical

---

# Purpose

The Subjects page serves as the central hub for all tutoring subjects offered by Success Path Mentors.

Its primary objectives are:

- Improve subject-related SEO rankings
- Help users quickly find the subject they need
- Drive traffic to individual subject pages
- Increase tutoring inquiries
- Build topical authority in education

---

# Business Goals

- Increase tutoring bookings
- Improve subject discoverability
- Increase organic traffic
- Improve conversion rate
- Support Programmatic SEO
- Strengthen internal linking

# Future Scalability

The page must allow adding unlimited subjects without changing the layout.

All cards should be generated from structured data.

No hardcoded content.

---

# Expected Outcome

The Subjects page should become the primary educational hub of the website, providing a highly discoverable, SEO-optimized, user-friendly experience that guides visitors toward the appropriate tutoring service while supporting long-term scalability and Programmatic SEO.
