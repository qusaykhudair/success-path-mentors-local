## 2. Evidence and decision rules

- **Observed:** current SPM titles, H1s, main content, grade/topic organization, forms and sitemap. Titles are copied exactly into the matrix; a related existing page is identified for new/HOLD candidates.
- **Inferred:** keyword wording, intent grouping, positioning, priority and potential cannibalization. These are management recommendations, not measured rankings or established traffic loss.
- **DATA REQUIRED:** search volume, keyword difficulty, country/language-specific demand, Search Console query/page history, conversions and revenue by service. All rows explicitly leave volume and difficulty unestimated.
- Competitor examples demonstrate current service/architecture patterns. Exact long-tail matches are labelled when not validated. Competitor publication alone does not justify copying its URL structure or adding an SPM service.
- P1: core service conversion or urgent scope verification; P2: useful existing-page improvements or the distinct exam-service gap; P3: narrower candidates and deferred work. A P1 HOLD remains HOLD. Priorities are qualitative, not volume-based.
- Funnel: TOFU = problem awareness, MOFU = comparison/evaluation, BOFU = inquiry/booking. Each row's primary keyword is the **target page's main owner**, so multiple grade/use-case rows can share it without assigning competing primary owners.

Competitor patterns used: [Oxford Learning subject directory](https://oxfordlearning.com/subject/) and [Oxford Learning programs](https://oxfordlearning.com/tutoring-programs/) separate subject, skill and program browsing. [Evolve Learning math tutoring](https://evolvelearning.ca/tutoring/math), [Evolve Learning chemistry tutoring](https://evolvelearning.ca/tutoring/chemistry) and [Evolve Learning physics tutoring](https://evolvelearning.ca/tutoring/physics) illustrate subject hubs that can accommodate school grades/course context.
