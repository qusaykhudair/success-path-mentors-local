## 3. Publishing rules

- Keep the UTM link exactly as written for the assigned post.
- Do not remove `utm_content`; it identifies the individual post variant.
- Do not use URL shorteners for the first activation test.
- Do not claim a physical center or local office.
- Do not guarantee marks, grades, exam outcomes or admissions.
- Do not imply that tutors complete homework on behalf of students.
- Prefer one clear CTA per post: **Learn more**, **Explore the program**, or **Book a free trial**.

## 4. Activation verification

After updating the Page Website/About field and publishing the first post:

1. Open the published Facebook link in a fresh browser session.
2. Confirm the intended Success Path Mentors canonical page loads.
3. Accept analytics consent for the controlled test visit.
4. Navigate to or click a tracked trial CTA.
5. Confirm GA4 / production analytics shows:
   - source = `facebook`
   - medium = `organic_social`
   - correct campaign
   - correct `utm_content`
   - intended landing path
6. Record a screenshot or analytics row as Phase 4.9 evidence.

## 5. Closure gate

Phase 4.9 can be closed only when all four are true:

- Page Website/About UTM link is live;
- at least one organic Facebook post with a unique UTM is live;
- destination/canonical resolution is correct;
- analytics attribution is verified.

Until then: **PHASE 4.9 = READY / EXTERNAL ACTIVATION PENDING**.
