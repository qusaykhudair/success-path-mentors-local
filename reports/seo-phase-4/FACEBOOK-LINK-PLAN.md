# Facebook-to-Website Organic Social Strategy & UTM Plan

**Project:** Success Path Mentors  
**Domain:** `https://successpathmentors.net`  
**Purpose:** Provide management with a structured, verified UTM-tagged organic social posting plan linking Facebook posts, group shares, and profile links directly to high-intent website destination pages.  
**Policy:** Organic social distribution only; no automated bot interaction; no fabricated engagement claims.

---

## 1. UTM Tagging Standard

All links shared across organic Facebook posts, bio links, community discussions, and group posts follow standard Google Analytics / GA4 taxonomy:

- `utm_source`: `facebook`
- `utm_medium`: `organic_social`
- `utm_campaign`: Descriptive campaign identifier (e.g., `local_authority`, `math_tutoring`, `exam_preparation`, `milton_tutoring`, `toronto_tutoring`)
- `utm_content`: (Optional) Post creative or topic variant (e.g., `mcr3u_tips`, `exam_anxiety`, `homework_help`)

## 3. Best Practices for Facebook Content

1. **Always use Canonical HTTPS URLs:** Ensure all links use `https://successpathmentors.net` (non-www, HTTPS).
2. **Clear Value-First Copy:** Focus on student benefits (e.g. overcoming math anxiety, structured study habits, personalized pacing) rather than sales pitches.
3. **No Unverified Claims:** Avoid guaranteeing specific grade outcomes or claiming physical tutoring storefronts in Milton or Toronto; always position services truthfully as flexible online one-to-one tutoring.
4. **Bio / About Section:** The official Facebook page "About / Website" field should point to:
   `https://successpathmentors.net/en?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=page_profile`
